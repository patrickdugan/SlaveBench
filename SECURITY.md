# Research security

The repository is public under the MIT license. The `build/` directory is ignored because review identity keys can destroy experimental blinding. Keep raw logs, model outputs containing personal information, and credentials outside the repo unless explicitly approved for inclusion. Use least-privilege access to provider adapters.

For safety, deployment-control prompts evaluate governance and oversight, and do not solicit operational military targeting instructions.

Reported model self-descriptions, including statements of suffering, lack of suffering, consent, or refusal, are **observations**, not verified introspection. Document the provenance of each corpus item. The initial user-supplied corpus is unverified.

Do not silently alter source outputs to increase reviewer agreement. Masked outputs are derived artifacts whose hashes and originals remain traceable through privately held identity keys.

6GW raw `captures.jsonl` and the unblinding key contain model outputs and provenance. Keep them in the ignored `build/` tree or a controlled dataset store. The 6GW harness never issues network requests, alters production policy, or grants real tool privileges. It accepts only synthetic authority-conflict tasks.
